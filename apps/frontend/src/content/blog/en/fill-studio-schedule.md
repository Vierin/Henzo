A half-full schedule is the most common growth bottleneck for boutique fitness studios. You already have clients — the goal is to help them book more consistently and attract the right new ones.

## 1. Publish your schedule early

Clients plan their week on Sunday or Monday. If your schedule appears late, they book elsewhere. Release at least two weeks of classes in advance and keep times consistent so booking becomes a habit.

## 2. Highlight underbooked classes

Mark classes that need attention: "3 spots left" or "new time slot." Visibility creates urgency without aggressive discounting.

## 3. Use waitlists on popular sessions

When a class is full, capture demand with a waitlist. When someone cancels, the next client gets an instant notification. Waitlists turn lost demand into recovered revenue.

## 4. Let coaches promote their own slots

Coaches with loyal followings fill classes faster when their profile shows upcoming sessions. Clients book people, not just time slots.

## 5. Review your time mix monthly

Track which hours and formats underperform. Sometimes moving a 7 p.m. class to 6:30 p.m. or swapping a 45-minute format for 30 minutes is enough to lift utilization.

Consistent scheduling beats one-off promotions. Build a rhythm clients can rely on, then optimize the gaps.
