Empty spots are expensive. Every no-show is lost revenue, a coach standing idle, and a client who could have taken the class. The good news: most no-shows are preventable with a few operational habits.

## Set clear booking expectations

Clients should know your cancellation window before they book. Display it on your schedule, in confirmation messages, and in your studio profile. A simple rule works best: cancel at least 12 hours ahead, or the spot counts as used.

## Use automated reminders

A reminder 24 hours before class and another 2 hours before start dramatically reduces forgetfulness. Automated push notifications beat manual messages because they are consistent and on time.

## Make rescheduling easy

If canceling feels like a hassle, clients simply disappear. Let them move to another slot in one tap instead of calling or messaging. Easy rescheduling keeps goodwill and fills your calendar.

## Track repeat offenders

Look for clients who miss classes often. A friendly follow-up message or a short note in your policy ("three no-shows in 30 days require prepayment") protects your schedule without punishing everyone.

## Fill gaps proactively

When a spot opens, notify clients on your waitlist immediately. Studios that react within minutes recover more revenue than those that wait until the class starts.

> Studios that combine reminders, clear policies, and fast waitlist fills typically see fewer empty classes within the first month.
