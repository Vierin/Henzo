Ho so studio la an tuong dau tien. Truoc khi den, khach quyet dinh ban co chuyen nghiep va dang thu khong.

## Mo dau bang gia tri ro rang

Hai cau dau tra loi: day gi, cho ai, ket qua gi. Tranh cau chung nhu "studio tot nhat". Cu the hon: "tap suc manh nhom nho cho nguoi ban ron".

## Dung anh that, khong dung stock

Hien khong gian, thiet bi va lop dang dien ra. Anh that tao niem tin nhanh hon. Cap nhat theo mua.

## Gioi thieu HLV

Bio ngan: chuyen mon, chung chi, phong cach. Khach chon ca nguoi day, khong chi khung gio.

## Giam buoc truoc khi dat

Ket thuc ho so bang hanh dong ro: xem lich, dat lop thu hoac lien he. Moi them mot buoc lam giam chuyen doi.

## Giu thong tin moi

Lich cu, gia cu hoac lien he sai lam mat niem tin. Kiem tra ho so hang thang.
