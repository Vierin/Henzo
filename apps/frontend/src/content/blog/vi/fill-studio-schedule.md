Lich day mot nua la nut that phat trien pho bien cua studio nho. Muc tieu la giup khach hien tai dat thuong xuyen hon va thu hut dung doi tuong moi.

## 1. Cong bo lich som

Nhieu nguoi len ke hoach tuan vao cuoi tuan. Neu lich ra muon, khach dat noi khac. Mo lich it nhat hai tuan truoc va giu khung gio on dinh.

## 2. Lam noi bat lop con cho

Nhan "con 3 cho" hoac "khung gio moi" tao cam giac can thiet ma khong can giam gia manh.

## 3. Dung danh sach cho

Khi lop day, thu thap nhu cau cho. Khi co huy, khach tiep theo nhan thong bao ngay — doanh thu duoc khoi phuc.

## 4. De HLV quang ba lop cua minh

HLV co fan rieng lap day lop nhanh hon khi ho so hien lich sap toi. Khach dat nguoi, khong chi dat gio.

## 5. Xem lai cau truc lich hang thang

Theo doi khung gio va dinh dang yeu. Doi 19h sang 18h30 hoac rut 45 phut con 30 phut doi khi du de tang lap day.

Nhip lich on dinh quan trong hon khuyen mai dot xuat. Xay thoi quen truoc, toi uu cho trong sau.
